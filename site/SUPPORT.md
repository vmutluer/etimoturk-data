### Frequently asked questions

**Does the app need an internet connection?**
No. EtimoTürk works 100% offline; the whole database (17,252 words, 1,353 root families, 12,446 shared words in 28 languages, 428 given names, 325 idioms, 291 place names) ships inside the app. Only purchasing or restoring Pro contacts the App Store.

**I can't find a word.**
Search resolves inflected forms ("kitaplarımızdan" → kitap). If a word is still missing, tap **Report missing** on the results screen; we try to add it in the next data update.

**I found an error in an etymology.**
Use **Report an error** under the word card, or open an issue in the data repository. Corrections go into the open dataset and ship with the next app update.

**What is EtimoTürk Pro? Is it a subscription?**
No subscription. Pro is a **one-time** purchase with Family Sharing enabled. It unlocks four tools: unlimited practice rounds and 60 Seconds, unlimited Text X-Ray (the first 40 words are free), Scan a Page with the camera, and the interactive Root Tree. Word search, cards, museum, atlas and the daily games stay free for everyone.

**How do I restore Pro on a new device?**
Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap **Restore Purchases** on the Pro screen or in Settings → About. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account; you are not charged again.

**I want a refund.**
In-app purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com

**How do I change the language?**
The **TR / EN** toggle at the top of the main screens switches the whole interface and content instantly.

**Why does the app ask for camera access?**
Only for **Scan a Page** (Pro). The image is processed on your device, never saved and never sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

**Where is my data?**
On your device. The app collects no data and has no server. Details: Privacy Policy.

**Widgets don't update.**
iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule; opening the app once usually helps. If the problem persists, remove and re-add the widget.
